IFRS Library: graduate projects

Loblaw paid about $12.4 billion for Shoppers Drug Mart in 2014, and $11.7 billion of that went to intangibles and goodwill rather than net tangible assets. Our five-person group took the deal apart under IFRS 3 and IAS 36 for a University of Windsor graduate course.

Where the purchase price went (fair value, C$ millions)

Total net assets acquired: $12,273 million. Prescription files and brands alone make up 68% of it.

What it did to the statements

  • An $800 million inventory step-up ran through cost of sales in 2014, squeezing gross margin.
  • A $3.5 billion term loan and $1.6 billion of notes funded the $6.7 billion cash portion, raising leverage and interest expense.
  • Amortisation, the step-up and deal costs diluted EPS in the short term, and the larger asset base lowered ROA and ROE.

Goodwill impairment test (IAS 36)

Loblaw's audited statements reported no goodwill impairment for the Retail cash-generating unit in the years after the deal. Pharmacy growth, the brand and the synergies kept the recoverable amount above the carrying amount.
